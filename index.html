◆ WHO WE ARE

A studio Nizamabad has trusted for sixteen years.

Samardha Photography — known locally as Samardha Digital Studio (సమర్ధ డిజిటల్ స్టూడియో) — has been shooting the city’s weddings, functions and portraits for over 16 years, from its studio on GG College Road, right by the Hanuman Temple in Shastri Nagar.

From muhurtham to reception, the team covers candid moments, traditional rituals, pre-wedding stories and event photography — with photo editing and album design handled in-house. Directories describe the studio simply: good packages for a memorable experience on a budget, delivered by a good team of photographers.
